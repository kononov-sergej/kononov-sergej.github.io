Privacy Policy

youtube-bot-v-razume · last updated: 15 November 2026

youtube-bot-v-razume (“the app”) is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel «В?Разуме» to manage his own channel. The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner’s permission (Google OAuth) the app accesses: the channel’s basic information, the list of its videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments), and the ability to upload videos, schedule them and edit video and channel metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, banner).

How the data is used

Only to perform actions the channel owner requests: uploading and scheduling his videos, editing their metadata and showing him statistics of his own videos. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token and the statistics are stored locally on the owner’s own computer. The data is not sold, not shared with and not transferred to any third party.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page. After revocation the app deletes the stored token at the next start; locally stored statistics can be deleted on request.
